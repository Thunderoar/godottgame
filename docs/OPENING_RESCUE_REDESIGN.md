- 2.30–4.25: turtle and rider travel together toward a warm, inhabited BESUT shore.
- 4.25–4.40: the turtle stops in the shallows before disembarkation.
- 4.40–5.15: Adam steps onto sand toward a water keeper's offered hand.
- 5.15–6.00: hold his safe arrival, the living turtle and two welcoming villagers.

The cold-to-warm sea/sky, detailed shell, unarmed wet/reaching/seated Adam poses,

anchors remain continuous; the turtle cannot depart before boarding or slide
while Adam transfers. No teleporting rescue or unexplained new boat is inserted.
